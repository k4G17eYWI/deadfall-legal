Deadfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: Deadfall has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. Your game stays on your device. The only thing the game sends is a crash report when something goes wrong, so we can fix it.

This policy covers the game Deadfall for Android ("the game"), published on Google Play. "We" means the developer of the game.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your characters, game progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the game's data in your Google account backup; that backup is run by Google under your settings, not by us.

2. Crash reports and diagnostics

When the game crashes, freezes or runs into an error, it sends a report to Sentry, the crash-reporting service we use. A report contains:

  • the error and the place in the game's code where it happened, with a short trail of the game's own technical log messages leading up to it; on an error, the recent lines of the system log written by the game and the graphics driver are attached;
  • the game version, and information about the device and system: model and manufacturer, Android version, memory and storage, screen, language and time zone;
  • a random identifier created for this installation of the game, used to count crash-free sessions per game version. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or any other identifier.

The game is set up not to attach personal information to reports. It does not send your name, contacts, location, photos or files. Like any internet service, Sentry sees the IP address of the connection that delivers a report; we do not use it to identify you.

Reports are sent encrypted (HTTPS), stored by Sentry in the European Union, and deleted automatically within 90 days. We use them only to find and fix bugs. We do not sell them or share them with anyone; Sentry processes them on our behalf as a service provider (Sentry's privacy policy).

3. What the game does not do

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no in-app purchases;
  • no analytics or tracking beyond the crash reports above;
  • no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

If this changes — for example, if we add ads or cloud saves — we will update this policy and the game's Google Play data safety section before that version is released.

4. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices and requests

Crash reports are not linked to you, so we cannot pick out the reports of a particular player; they are deleted automatically within 90 days. You can stop all reporting by uninstalling the game. For any question or request about your data, write to us at deadfallrpg@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
